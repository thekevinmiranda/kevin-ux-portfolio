A little about me

I design the quiet parts that make products feel effortless.

After years inside product teams at fintech and health startups, I went independent to do the work I care about most — the slow, deliberate kind.

I partner with a handful of clients at a time, from first sketch to shipped release. That means I stay close to the details: the empty states, the loading moments, the way a button feels under your thumb. Good design should disappear — you only notice it when it's missing.

When I'm not in Figma you'll find me shooting 35mm around the old town, or teaching a Sunday typography workshop for junior designers.
